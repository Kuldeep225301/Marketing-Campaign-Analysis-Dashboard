# Marketing Campaign Analysis Dashboard
## 📌 Project Overview
The primary goal of this Marketing Campaign Analysis Dashboard is to provide marketing leadership with a consolidated, data-driven view to evaluate channel profitability, analyze campaign ROI, and optimize global budget allocation across digital and traditional media formats.

---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions Addressed
- Which marketing channels generate the highest return on investment (ROI)?
- Are traditional advertising formats (e.g., TV Commercials) still cost-effective compared to digital media?
- How is marketing revenue distributed across key geographical regions?
- How can the marketing team reallocate budget to lower customer acquisition spend and increase net revenue?

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Revenues:** $42.54M
- **Total Spend:** $26.00M
- **Average ROI:** 0.68
- **Total Clicks:** 10M
- **Top Performing Campaign:** Influencer Marketing (ROI: 1.10)

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Root Cause Analysis
1. **High Digital ROI:** **Influencer Marketing** leads all channels with a peak ROI of **1.10** at a spend of $3.1M, followed by **Social Media Ads** (ROI: 0.66).
2. **Traditional Media Underperformance:** **TV Commercials** generate a low ROI of **0.45** despite absorbing nearly $3M in campaign budget.
3. **Regional Revenue Breakdown:** Revenue generation is strong across global markets, led by **Africa ($8.6M)**, **North America ($7.5M)**, and **Asia ($7.4M)**.
4. **Root Cause:** Capital is over-allocated to legacy traditional advertising channels with low engagement and sub-1.0 ROI performance.

---

## 💡 Recommendations & Action Plan
- **Reallocate Budget:** Shift **30% to 40%** of underperforming budget from **TV Commercials** directly into high-converting digital channels (**Influencer Marketing** and **Social Media Ads**).
- **Regional Focus:** Increase targeted ad spends in top revenue-generating markets (**Africa** and **North America**).
- **Campaign Optimization:** Re-evaluate targeting and ad copy for Email Marketing and Billboards to lift their overall ROI above the 1.0 threshold.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Stack & Tools Used
- **BI Tool:** Power BI Desktop
- **ETL & Data Cleaning:** Power Query
- **Data Modeling:** Star Schema / Relational Data Model
- **Calculations:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
